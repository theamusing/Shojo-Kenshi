PARRY (parry.wav)
20 Sword Sound Effects (Attacks and Clashes)
Author: StarNinjas
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/20-sword-sound-effects-attacks-and-clashes
Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/sword_clash_-_starninjas_0.zip
File: sword_clash.1.ogg
Preserved as: source-assets/audio/parry.ogg

Game edits: mono conversion, resampling, leading silence removal, filtering,
short fades, level normalization, shortened hit tails and quiet parry early reflections.
Hit uses a final 4.8kHz second-order low-pass to retain cutting detail
at the game's slowed playback rate, while keeping the wet layer and subdued tail.
See manifest.json for per-file provenance, hashes and processing notes.
Rebuild with scripts/prepare-sfx.py using the five preserved inputs in source-assets/audio.
The source-assets directory is for offline editing and is not published to GitHub Pages.

The game's existing original synthesized BGM is separate from these recordings.
